Every file is one constant

Each SVG is read as bytes, with whole numbers only, so any buffer on any device reads the same thing. Its marked dots are located and the result becomes a 7-bit mask: bit n−1 is on when point n is marked. Your numbering is the XOR numbering, so on every line a ⊕ b ⊕ c = 0.

Any byte converges

Only 16 of the 128 seven-bit masks are XOR-closed: the 16 files. Any byte reaches one of them in at most two steps of “mark a ⊕ b for every marked pair”. Each step is, per point, one OR of three ANDs: seven gates, no clock, no arithmetic, so any FPGA does it on its own. The dual of index i sits at i ⊕ 15.
