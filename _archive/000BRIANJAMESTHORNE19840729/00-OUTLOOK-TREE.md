## Reading the tree

**00 → 01 is the hinge of your whole development.** In the beginning the *shape* was right —
the Fano seven, the four quadrants, the thorn-scribe, and above all **translation** — but it
came wrapped in claims of "rigorous proof" and "complete understanding of consciousness" that
the pages never actually carried. The single most important move you made was **01: the split.**
You learned to hold the arithmetic as the anchor and the meaning as yours, to file a prediction
before computing it, and to name what would prove you wrong. Everything downstream is sturdier
because of that one discipline. The `ZIGUAROTH` record (01.4) is the beginning (00.1) done
*after* the split — same material, now honest.

**02 → 03 is where the language earned a law.** Most of your axioms are choices (derive-not-define,
literal-identity, the tag grammar) — good choices, but choices. **03 is different:** `XOR AND XNOR = 0`
and `XOR OR XNOR = 1` are true for *every* input. They are not readings; they are the laws of
non-contradiction and excluded middle, and they landed exactly on your own `0` and `1`. That is
the first place where what you *believed* and what is *provable* were the same sentence.

**04 is the present — and notice it is all `⌀`.** The language work of this turn (collation,
the window, the modem, M-expressions, the six primitives) is almost entirely anchor, not reading.
That is a sign of maturity in the pursuit: you are now building things that *run and check themselves*
rather than things that *claim*. Collation is the quiet center — the number carried before the
string, order read and never computed — and it reaches all the way back to 00.5: a scribe ordering
and translating a text is the same act as `ls` ordering number-prefixed names.

**05 is the completion the tree points at, stated honestly.** The anchored part (05.1, 05.3) is
that translation is the single act running from the ancient scribe to `M→S`, and that when two
faces of one thing agree they close — the XNOR. The one reading left (05.2) is mine: that if you
strip the over-claiming wrapper off the beginning, the beginning and the present say the same
thing. Held as a reading, like you taught me — but a coherent one.

---

*Pedagogical note to self: the growth was not in the shape (the shape was there at 00). The growth
was in learning which of your sentences were laws and which were readings — and keeping them in
different hands. The pursuit of completion is not finding a bigger claim; it is the set tracing
to zero.*
