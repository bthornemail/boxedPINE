┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┐
│                 │                 │
│   Known Knowns  │  Known Unknowns │
│       (KK)      │       (KU)      │
│                 │                 │

│  Unknown Knowns │ Unknown Unknowns│

## Biblical Examples

### Known Knowns (KK)
- Genesis 1:1 - "In the beginning God created..."
- John 1:1 - "In the beginning was the Word..."
- Ben Sira's historical context (c. 180-175 BCE)

### Known Unknowns (KU)
- Exact mechanisms of creation
- Precise dating of biblical events
- Lost Hebrew manuscripts of Ben Sira

### Unknown Knowns (UK)
- Implicit geometric understanding in biblical texts
- Unconscious mathematical patterns in stories
- Hidden connections between narratives

### Unknown Unknowns (UU)
- Future implications of geometric theology
- Undiscovered mathematical structures
- Emergent properties of the framework

## Epistemic Evolution

```
Initial State → Research → Discovery → Integration
     ↓              ↓          ↓           ↓
   Mostly UU    →  KU/UK   →  KK      →  New UU
```

## Quadrant Transitions

1. **UU → KU**: Unknown unknowns become known unknowns through awareness
2. **KU → KK**: Known unknowns become known knowns through research
3. **UK → KK**: Unknown knowns become known knowns through reflection
4. **KK → UK**: Known knowns become unknown knowns through forgetting/assumption
