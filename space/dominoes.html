Dominoes

The extended slide rule as a board. A repetend's digits, two to a domino, each digit in the Aegean set of its place (the five sets cycled); a lexeme's bytes, a byte to a domino, high and low nibble, its 8-dot Braille cell. Drag tiles onto the board; the order you lay them is the path, and the path plays as a text track, one cue a domino, to every room. Drag the empty board to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom, double-click a domino to turn it, drag it back to the tray to lift it.
