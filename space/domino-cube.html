Domino Cube

A word's UTF-8 bytes, three at a time, are cells of the 256 × 256 × 256 cube; each domino joins a cell to the next, the last back to the first, so the word closes into a loop. One domino is laid on each frame of the step (half a second, thirty to a turn). Every face is the step.svg, with the loop seen along its axis: ±x, ±y, ±z. Drag to turn the cube.
