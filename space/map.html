The colour wheel

The 144 synsets of WordNet's color (proofs I-199) as agents: hue is the angle, depth in WordNet the distance out. Light Garden's eight characters stand on their hues. In 2D deeper synsets lie on top; in 2.5D depth lifts them. Each steps K on its own beat; speakers on the agent bridge join and walk to a colour they name.
